StreetSense · Methodology

Report a problemRepair tracker

Cities cannot fix what they cannot see.

StreetSense turns ordinary road images into mapped, ranked repair tasks: detect earlier, prioritise smarter, repair faster. This page shows exactly how it decides, what it can and cannot do yet, and where the data comes from.

The pipeline

Image input
Street photos, dashcam or phone footage, fleet feeds.
Preprocess to 640 px
Every image is resized before detection.
YOLO11n detection
Finds and classifies defects, with a confidence score on each.
Incident mapping
Each confirmed defect becomes a location-tagged record on the map (phone GPS or a map pin).
Prioritisation
A published score ranks urgent issues first.
Review & repair tracking
Work orders, crews, before/after photos, metrics.

Human in the loop

Every AI detection waits in a review queue.

Uncertain detections are shown to the reviewer first. Because baseline precision is about 70%, no result triggers a repair without a person confirming it.
