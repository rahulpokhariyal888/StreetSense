The street-to-solution loop

Three steps. Nothing to chase.

Every useful report gives a maintenance team a better starting point.

  1. 01

    Pinpoint it

    Name the street, or let your phone share your location.

  2. 02

    Show it

    Add a clear photo so the reviewer sees what you see. Photos can be analysed by a road-defect model, but a person always decides.

  3. 03

    Follow it

    Open “My reports” to see whether it is submitted, under review, assigned or resolved.
