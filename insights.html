Scored incidents

Condition index 100 = perfect, 0 = failed (PCI-style bands). Based on defect type, extent and road speed, not on model confidence. Depth/width/speed are estimated unless recorded.

Human review queue

Nothing the AI finds becomes an incident, a work order or a public repair until a person confirms it. Uncertain detections come first. Confirming creates the work order with its repair deadline.

Scan dashcam or phone footage

Upload a video of a drive. YOLO checks a frame every few seconds, merges repeat sightings of the same defect, and creates incidents (with the flagged frame as evidence) and repair work orders with deadlines.
