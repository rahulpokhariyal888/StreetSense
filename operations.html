        The StreetSense workflow

        From street evidence to civic action

        A human-in-the-loop pipeline. Software suggests; people decide.

        1. 01
          Capture

          Photos from crews and citizens, or a field report with a location.

        2. 02
          Classify

          If a trained road-defect model is installed it suggests a defect class and confidence. Without one, the photo is stored and flagged for manual review.

        3. 03
          Verify & prioritise

          A reviewer confirms the defect and sets severity from type, extent and road-user impact.

        4. 04
          Dispatch

          A verified incident becomes a work order with a crew and a due date. Status flows back to the register.

        Model output is decision support and must be checked by an authorised reviewer before any municipal action.
